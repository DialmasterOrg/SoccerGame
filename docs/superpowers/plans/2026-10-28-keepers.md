# Keepers (7c, plan 2 of 5) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** From the School Field on, a computer keeper guards each goal, Harrison's own and the opponent's. Each one positions himself, dives, catches or pushes the ball away, holds it and drop-kicks it out. The Energy Wave beats him, and tricks fool him when he comes out. CI measures him against the spec's targets. The Backyard and the Park play exactly as before.

**Architecture:** A keeper is a `Keeper` record that rides on an ordinary `Player` body, as Biscuit does. The keepers live in `World.keepers`, which is null on the small stage, so nothing changes there. The code splits into three modules, after `dog.ts` and `dogAi.ts`:
- `keeper.ts` is his body system and data: profiles by difficulty and level, his acts, the hold and the drop kick.
- `keeperAi.ts` is his brain: where he stands, reading shots, coming out.
- `keeperSaves.ts` decides catch or push away.

`stepWorld` steps both keepers itself, so every caller (the match scene, every scripted tuning player) still passes only Harrison's and the opponent's intents. The art draws three made-up keepers on the shared body with gloves, adds new keeper poses, and turns the dive frames along the goal mouth. `keeperView.ts` draws them in the match only once the parent has approved the sheet and the GIF.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript 7, Vite 8, Vitest 4, Canvas 2D, Node 20, tsx; plain `python3` with PIL for the GIF.

## Global Constraints

- **Keepers only on a stage with `keepers: true`,** that is the School Field and every later venue. The Backyard and the Park have none and play exactly as before. Every existing test passes unchanged, including plan 1's AI-vs-AI matches on `SCHOOL_STAGE` with no lineup. The only existing tests that may change are ones that build a `Stage` or `BodyExtras` literal, which gain the new field.
- **The guarantees still hold.** Every guarantee Plans 2–10 made holds with keepers: bodies and the ball stay inside the field, never in a goal frame or its roof, and never get stuck. The one exception is a keeper blown into his net by the Energy Wave.
- **Harrison doesn't change.** His speeds, jump and kick power stay as they are, and so do the ball physics.
- **The box.** A keeper never leaves his own penalty box: `markings.box`, 110 out from the goal line, z 10–118.
- **Difficulty.** Harrison's keeper always plays at Normal, at the match's keeper level. The difficulty setting, mid-match included, changes only the opponent's keeper.
- **No fouls on keepers.** Nobody trips a keeper. Harrison's steal and slides do nothing to a keeper holding the ball. There are no throws or rolled passes, and a drop kick is never a shot on goal.
- **Text and assets.** On-screen text uses only uppercase letters, digits and `' - : ! ? . , / + < >`, with no parentheses. No image, audio or font files. No surname anywhere; the keepers are made up.
- **Approvals.** The keepers' sheet and GIF go to **the parent alone** with `SendUserFile`, because Harrison wants to be surprised. **Task 10, drawing keepers in the match, waits for the parent's approval.** Ask before any merge into `main` and before any push.
- **Git.** Work on branch `plan-7c-school-field` (from main `a76aa84`). Run `git status --short` before every commit. Commits follow Conventional Commits, 50/72, with no AI attribution trailers.
- **Commands.** Run tests from the project root: one file with `npx vitest run <file>`, the whole suite with `npx vitest run`, the types with `npx tsc --noEmit`, the build with `npm run build`.
- **Fixtures.** School Field matches have no catalog entries until plan 3, so tests reach them through `CareerMatch` fixtures with `venue: 'school'`. The existing `SCHOOL_TEST` in `matchScene.test.ts` is `backyard-1` moved to the School: tryout 1, against Max.
- **Plan 1's deferred minors stay deferred.** None of the three touches anything this plan changes: the speed lines under the doubled HUD rows, the dangling JSDoc above `METER` in `powerView.ts`, and the overlay composited at view size.
- **Bands and dials.** The keepers' dials and the tests' scripts, seeds and bands are the builder's, but every band comes from the spec's targets. If no setting of the dials meets a target without breaking a rule above, stop and report the numbers to the parent. Don't widen the band.

## Review Focus

1. **A shot deflected on its way** (off a body, a post or the bar): the keeper reads the new flight after his reaction time, and never dives at the old line. Pinned in Task 3.
2. **A kickoff while a keeper is busy** (holding, diving, lying down or mid-drop-kick, after a goal or as golden goal starts): everything resets. No held ball, no climbing kick, and every keeper is set in his goal. Pinned in Task 5.
3. **Harrison next to a holding keeper**, pressed against him or standing where the drop kick goes: the kick still climbs over him, and he can't charge it down or win it. Pinned in Task 5.
4. **A loose ball just outside the box**, or on its edge, with Max going for it: the keeper stops at the edge and never leaves the box, and nothing sticks. Pinned in Task 6.
5. **A difficulty change from the pause menu during a dive:** only the opponent's keeper changes, his dive plays out, and Harrison's keeper is untouched. Pinned in Task 8.

- **The kids.** Three made-up kids, as varied in hair and skin as the Park crew, each with a head in all three facings. All three use `HARRISON_BODY`, the same physics body as every player.
- **Their tops.** Each keeper's top is unlike any kit in the game: no `KITS` or `HARRISON_KITS` jersey colour, and no two keepers alike. All three wear gloves. Eddie's top is the builder's suggestion: an emerald green, clearly brighter and bluer than the grass. Add the master colours the tops need, and raise the cap in `colors.test.ts` to the new count on purpose, with a comment that names the keepers. It is 61 colours today, capped at 64.
- **The side-view poses,** on the shared body via `poseKey`:
  - ready: a crouch, gloves out in front
  - shuffle: crouched side steps
  - catch: gloves meeting the ball at the chest
  - push: a palm or fist swatting it away
  - hold: the ball clutched at `KEEPER_HANDS`
  - dropKick: drop, swing, strike, follow through
  - getUp: lying, kneeling, standing
  - smother: a low dive forward along his facing, at the ball at a dribbler's feet
- **Dives run up or down the screen, not sideways** (the spec's *Keeper art*). Build them from a flat dive pose, drawn with `drawFigure`: torso about 90°, arms stretched past his head, gloves leading, legs trailing.
  - Toward the far post: `turnLeft` that figure. Head and gloves point up, and his chest faces the pitch.
  - Toward the near post: mirror the figure, then turn it the same way. Head and gloves point down, and his chest still faces the pitch.
  - Then `shearUp(…, DEPTH_SHEAR)`, so his length lies along the goal mouth as the camera draws depth.
  - For facing −1, mirror before turning and shear after, because depth always leans right.
  - The three frames are launch, full stretch and landed.
  - If the parent's verdict calls for another approach, that is the builder's to find.
- Every frame is outlined, in logical colours only.

- Produces (in `keeperAi.ts`):
  ```ts

  export const NEW_FLIGHT = { px: 40, share: 0.15 };
  export const NARROW = { far: 320, near: 110 };
  export const BACKPEDAL_SHARE = 0.4;
  export function setSpot(w: World, k: Keeper): { x: number; z: number };
  export function readShot(w: World, k: Keeper): ShotRead | null;
  export function thinkKeeper(w: World, k: Keeper, dt: number): KeeperIntent;
  ```

- Produces (in `keeper.ts`): an `intent.dive` starts a dive. The act becomes `'dive'`, `k.dive` takes the direction and `ev.dived` the side. His body slides `diveTravel` along it over `diveS`, moved by the keeper module, not by `stepPlayer`. Then he's `'down'` for `downS`, `'getUp'` for `getUpS`, then `'set'` again. Bodies keep him out of the goal throughout.

**The brain's rules:**
- **A new flight.** The ball's ground velocity changes between ticks by more than `max(NEW_FLIGHT.px, NEW_FLIGHT.share × its speed)`: a kick, a touch, a deflection, a bounce off a post, or a hand-set ball in a test. Then `readS = reactionS`, and he draws `errZ = posErrPx × (2·rng − 1)`. Until `readS` runs out, he keeps his last intent.
- **The set spot.** He stands on the line from his goal's centre to the ball, at x = lineX + (toward the pitch) × (PLAYER_R + off).
  - off = `SET_OFF + comeOutPx × closeness`
  - closeness = `clamp01((NARROW.far − d) / (NARROW.far − NARROW.near))`, where d is the ball's distance from his goal's centre.
  - z = `cz + (ball.z − cz) × (PLAYER_R + off) / max(|ball.x − lineX|, PLAYER_R + off)`, plus `errZ`, clamped between his posts (z0 + PLAYER_R to z1 − PLAYER_R) and into his box.
- **A read.** `readShot` returns where the ball will cross his x-plane: the time t > 0, its depth, and its height (under gravity, ignoring drag and bounces). It does so only when the ball is coming toward his goal and `onTarget(ball, his goal)`; otherwise it returns null.
- **Answering a read,** once `readS` has run out:
  - If `read.y − BALL_R > reachY`, he can't take it where he stands, so he backs toward his line at `BACKPEDAL_SHARE` of his speed. Task 6's chip test pins this.
  - Else, if he can get within `PLAYER_R + reachZ` of `read.z` on his feet in `read.t`, he shuffles to `read.z`.
  - Else he dives toward it: `dirZ = sign(read.z − z)`, `dirX = 0`.
- **No read:** he walks to his set spot.
- **Outside play:** no move and no dive.

**The rules:**
- **When he can play:** `canPlay` holds when his body is on the ground and not fooled, and his act is `'set'` or `'rush'` (his standing zone) or `'dive'` (his dive zone).
- **His standing zone:** within `PLAYER_R + reachZ + BALL_R` of him in depth, `PLAYER_R + BALL_R + 3` along the pitch, and up to `reachY + BALL_R` high.
- **His dive zone:** within `BALL_R + 3` of the segment from his centre out to `diveReach` along the dive, and up to `reachY + BALL_R` high. In a smother (a dive mostly along x) it reaches only 12 high, and what a smother reaches he always holds.
- **Swept:** each zone is tested along the ball's path this tick, so no shot tunnels through.
- **The odds,** `holdChance`:
  - It is 1 when `speed ≤ SOFT_SPEED` and `reach ≤ 0.5`.
  - Otherwise it is `holdOdds − 0.8 × max(0, speed − catchSpeed) / catchSpeed − 0.4 × reach`, less 0.25 for a PERFECT strike, and less 0.2 for a volley or header struck within 120 px of his goal line. It is clamped to 0..1.
  - `reach` is |dz| / (PLAYER_R + reachZ) when he's standing, and distance along the dive / (diveTravel + diveReach) in a dive.
  - The four constants are starting values.
- **The save:** if `rng() < holdChance`, he holds the ball (`startHold`); otherwise he pushes it away.
- **Pushing it away,** never toward his goal:
  - A ball at or above `barY − 8` is tipped over: up fast enough to clear the bar before the line, carrying a little toward the goal, onto the roof or the end fence, which bounce it back as today.
  - From a dive, it goes wide: along the dive in depth and out from goal, at 0.35–0.5 of its pace.
  - Standing, it goes back out along the pitch, with a spread in depth.
  - A push sets `ev.pushed` and `touchedBy`, and sets `pose = push` when he's standing.
- **When he can't play,** the ball bounces off his body: `blockBall(k.body, ball, field)`.
- **The pose.** A standing catch's or push's `pose` shows for 0.25 s; `stepKeeper` counts it down.

  - `tricks.ts`: `squaredUp(w: World, d: Player = w.away): boolean` and `seesTrick(w: World, d: Player = w.away): boolean`.

**The rules:**
- **The wave.** On each live tick, and once per wave, the beam may reach the opponent's keeper. The test is the one Max's uses (the lateral, ahead and reached checks), and it runs whatever Max's outcome was.
  - He's blown back along the beam (`blowAway`) and lands in his net. `inNet` is set when his centre enters the goal box, and the goal then holds him in.
  - His hold, dive and punt are cleared, and the ball carries on with the beam. `power.blown` is set.
  - Nobody braces against it in this plan. Gus's brace is plan 5's.
- **Walking out of the net.** Once he's on his feet, his brain walks him straight out toward the pitch, in any phase, the goal pause included. `inNet` clears once he's clear of the goal box.
- **Tricks.** At the sell, the opponent's keeper counts as a defender too, but only while his act is `'rush'` (coming out).
  - Squared up, he's fooled like an outfield opponent: the difficulty's window and times at the allRound scale.
  - If he only saw it, he's wise to it.
  - `tickDefender` counts down his fooled and wise timers too.
- **A fooled keeper:**
  - Confused (a stepover): he dives in depth toward his sway side at once, `dirZ = sign(fooled.swayZ − z)`, the wrong way.
  - Stumbling (a flick): `canPlay` is false and he stands until it ends.
  - Either way he's wise for `WISE_S` after.

**The drills.** Each trial draws its staging from its own seeded generator, and Harrison's shots carry his real aim error (`HUMAN_AIM_ERROR_DEG`). Each one ends in a goal, a save (the keeper caught or pushed it), wide, or 3 s:

- **corner:** 50–100 px out, z 30–98, aimed at the corner further from the keeper, at a charge of 0.7–0.95.
- **oneOnOne:** Harrison on the ball 150 px out at z 64 ± 20, the keeper set, Max parked. He dribbles in and then waits with the ball at his feet. By the seed's turn, he does one of three things:

  - chip him: once the keeper is coming out and 30–60 px off his line;
  - step over him: once he's squared up (tricks at the difficulty).
  If the keeper doesn't commit within 1.5 s, he shoots for the far corner.
- **roller:** a ball rolled straight at the set keeper (his z ± 2) at 60–200 px/s from 20–100 px out, at either keeper.

**`keeperFrame`'s rules,** in this order:
- **Blown or fooled:** his body's clip (`body.anim`).
- **`'dive'`:** a smother (|dirX| > |dirZ|) shows the smother pose; otherwise a far dive if `dirZ > 0` and a near one if not. The frame runs launch to full stretch on `actT / diveS`.
- **`'down'`:** the last frame of his dive or smother.
- **`'getUp'`:** the getUp pose on `actT / getUpS`.
- **`'hold'`:** `catch` while `pose` says so, then `hold`.
- **`'kick'`:** dropKick on `actT`.
- **`'set'`:** `push` while `pose` says so; shuffle (looping) while moving faster than 5 px/s; ready (looping) otherwise.
- **`'rush'`:** his body's clip (run or sprint).

A dive's sprite is drawn at `screen(x, lift, z)` minus its anchor, with `lift = 4 × sin(π × actT / diveS)` during the dive and 0 lying down.
